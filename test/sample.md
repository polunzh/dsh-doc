# PET（B1）口语回答

## Part 1 · 常考问题

**1. What subject do you find the easiest at school? (Why?)**

A：I think Art is the easiest subject for me. I don't need to remember lots of facts. I love drawing and being creative, so the lessons feel relaxing.

B：For me, PE is the easiest. I like moving my body and playing games with classmates. It's not difficult and I always have fun.

**2. What kind of music do you listen to?**

A：I mostly listen to pop music. It is upbeat and makes me feel happy. I usually listen to it on my way to school. Sometimes I also enjoy soft music when I want to relax.

B：I like gentle classical music best. It helps me calm down when I am busy with homework. I don't like really loud music.

**3. What type of animals do you like?**

A：I like cats the most. They are quiet and cute. They like to stay with you and they are easy to look after at home.

B：I love dolphins. They are very clever and friendly. I hope I can see them in the sea one day.

**4. Do you like shopping? (Why?/Why not?)**

A：Yes, I do. I enjoy shopping with my friends. We can look for new clothes and interesting things together. But I don't like shopping for a very long time.

B：Not really. Shopping is tiring for me. I prefer to stay at home reading books. If I need something, my parents can buy it online.

**5. How often do you take photos? (Why?/Why not?)**

A：I take photos about two or three times a week. I love taking photos of nice views and my friends. It helps me keep wonderful memories.

B：I don't take photos very often. I prefer just enjoying things with my eyes instead of taking pictures all the time.

**6. Tell us about the kind of films you like.**

A：I like adventure films best. The stories are exciting and full of surprises. The main characters are brave, and I always feel excited when I watch them.

B：I prefer funny comedy films. They make me laugh a lot when I feel stressed. I usually watch them with my family at weekends.

**7. Where do you like to meet your friends?**

A：I like meeting my friends in the city park. It's quiet and we can walk, chat or play games there. Sometimes we also meet in the library to study together.

B：I like meeting my friends at the shopping café. We can buy drinks and talk about our school life comfortably.

**8. Do you enjoy playing games on phones and computers?**

A：Yes, I do, but only for a short time. Some online games are fun and I can play with my friends. However, I know I shouldn't play too much because it's bad for my eyes.

B：No, not really. I think playing screen games is boring. I'd rather play ball games outside with my friends.

## Part 2 · 备用问题

**1. Do you find maths easy at school? (Why?/Why not?)**

A：Yes, I do. I like working with numbers. It is fun to solve difficult problems and I usually get good marks.

B：No, I don't. Sometimes the problems are hard. I spend lots of time practising, but I still make mistakes.

**2. Do you listen to music? What do you listen to?**

A：Yes, I listen to music every day. I love pop music best. It makes me feel relaxed after school.

B：Not very often. When I listen, I choose soft classical music. It helps me focus on my homework.

**3. Do you like animals? Which ones?**

A：Yes, I really like animals. My favourite are cats. They are lovely and quiet, and they are good pets.

B：Yes, I do. I like dolphins. They are clever and friendly. I hope I can watch them one day.

**4. Is shopping fun?**

A：Yes, it is fun when I go with my friends. We look at interesting things together. But I don't like shopping for hours.

B：Not really for me. It is tiring. I prefer reading books at home.

**5. Do you like taking photos?**

A：Yes, I love it. I take photos of my family and the places I visit. Every photo reminds me of a happy day.

B：Not really. I would rather enjoy the moment than hold a phone all the time.
